The problem

Physical invoices for the VIP Industries corporate card get lost between the purchase and the accounts department. Every statement cycle ends in a manual hunt: match paper to line items, scan, name files, email one by one.

The fix

Photograph the bill the moment you get it and send it to a Telegram bot. It reads the bill, matches it to the exact card transaction already parsed from your HDFC email alerts, files it against the right statement cycle, and — on your approval — emails accounts one clean, indexed PDF bundle.

Constraint · cron slots

Vercel's Hobby plan caps you at 2 cron jobs, once-daily. /api/cron/sync and /api/cron/monthly-report already occupy both. Receipt Rail needs three more schedules.

Fix without upgrading: replace both entries with one /api/cron/tick dispatcher that runs daily and branches on the date — sync and nudge always, statement reconciliation on the 18th, submission build on the 23rd. One slot, five jobs, and every job stays independently testable via ?job=.

Constraint · Supabase key

The app currently ships only SUPABASE_ANON_KEY. A private receipts bucket cannot be written with the anon key under sane RLS, and the cron already reads all users' rows — which today works only because RLS is effectively open.

Do this in Phase 0: add SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY (server env only, never NEXT_PUBLIC_), turn RLS on for receipts/submissions, and route all storage + cron writes through the admin client.

Failure handling — every branch has a defined end state

FailureBehaviour
Vision call errors / rate-limitedExponential backoff, 3 tries; then row stays pending and the daily job retries. Chat says "couldn't read it — I'll retry".
Image unreadable / not a billdoc_type='other' → bot asks "Is this a receipt? Enter amount + date manually" with a modal-style prompt.

Why not just email receipts to the app?

You could forward bills to a Gmail alias and let the existing sync pick them up. It fails on the thing that matters: no feedback loop. Email can't tell you "this didn't match, pick one of these two" in the two seconds you're still standing at the counter, and it can't nudge you at 8pm about the three charges still bare.

Chat is the right medium because reconciliation is a conversation, not a drop-box.

Prompt rules that earn their keep

Total ≠ subtotal. Always return the amount actually charged to the card — the grand total after tax, tip and rounding. That is the only number the bank alert will agree with.
Never invent. Anything illegible goes in unreadable_fields and stays null. A null the bot asks about is cheap; a hallucinated GSTIN in a filing is not.
Indian formats. DD/MM/YYYY, lakh/crore digit grouping, "Rs."/"₹"/"INR", GSTIN regex validation, CGST+SGST split.
Card tail is gold. If the slip prints the last 4 digits, extraction confidence and match score both jump — it proves the bill belongs to the company card, not your personal one.

Deterministic safety net after the model returns

Arithmetic check
subtotal + tax ≈ total (±₹1) else drop confidence 0.2
GSTIN checksum
15-char format + state code validation; invalid → null it
Date sanity
Future dates or >120 days old → flag for confirmation
Currency guard
Non-INR → convert flag raised, never silently matched
Where the model choice lands: accuracy is the priority, so every receipt is read twice by two different model families and conflicts are adjudicated by a third. Full strategy and costs on the next page.

07 · AccuracyModel strategy when errors are unacceptable

You can't buy 100% from a model. You can engineer it.

The honest position. No vision model returns 100% on creased thermal slips, faded ink and handwritten tips — not Claude, not GPT, not a dedicated OCR service. Anyone quoting 100% is quoting a benchmark, not your shoebox. But 100% accuracy at the point of submission is achievable, because every rupee in the package is checked against HDFC's own statement and anything that doesn't reconcile blocks the send. Accuracy is a property of the pipeline, not the model.

Four layers, each catching what the one before it missed

L1
Two models, independently
Every receipt read by Sonnet 5 and GPT-5.6 Terra in parallel. Agreement on total + date + merchant is strong evidence; disagreement is a flag, not a coin toss.
L2
Tiebreak on conflict
They disagree → Opus 5 adjudicates. Fires on ~15% of receipts, so the flagship price is paid only where it earns its keep.
L3
Deterministic checks
Arithmetic tie-out, GSTIN/VAT checksum, currency sanity, date plausibility. Code, not a model — it never has an off day.
L4
The statement
The real guarantee. Every amount must equal a line on HDFC's statement. Misread ₹3,400 as ₹8,400 and no statement line matches — the cycle blocks, the discrepancy is named in chat, you fix it in one tap.
The bank is the ground truth, and nothing reaches accounts without agreeing with it to the rupee. That is a stronger promise than any model benchmark — and it is the layer that actually delivers what you asked for.

Two decisions worth stating plainly

You already hold both keys. OpenAI is genuinely cheaper at the small-model tier — Luna's $0.20/$1.20 undercuts everything. At the flagship tier it reverses: Terra's $2/$12 is marginally dearer than Sonnet 5's $2/$10. Using both anyway, price stops deciding and consensus becomes the point.
Leave lib/sync.js alone. Its prompt is tuned in production for refund detection, merchant canonicalisation and dedupe. Swapping models to consolidate providers buys nothing and risks a missed refund landing in a submission — and split providers mean one outage cannot stop the pipeline.

08 · GlobalForeign currency & non-Indian receipts

Your Europe trip breaks naive matching — here's the fix

The core problem. A €48.50 dinner in Lisbon does not appear on your HDFC alert as €48.50, or as any round conversion of it. It appears as ₹4,7xx — Visa's wholesale rate, plus HDFC's ~3.5% foreign-currency markup, plus 18% GST on that markup, posted 1–3 days later than the date printed on the bill. Exact-amount matching, which is 40 of the 100 match points, fails on every single one of your ~20 European receipts.

How a foreign charge is reconstructed

1
Extract in origin currency
Model returns total: 48.50, currency: "EUR", country: "PT" — never converted by the model.
2
Fetch the day's rate
ECB daily reference rate for the receipt date via Frankfurter — free, no key. Cached in fx_rates, fetched once per currency-day.
3
Model the markup band
expected₹ = €48.50 × rate × [1.000 … 1.060]
network spread ~0.5–1% + markup 3.5% + 18% GST on markup.
4
Score against the band
Inside band → full amount points. Within 1.5% outside → partial. The learned markup for your card tightens the band over time.
Worked example. €48.50 · 12 Jun · ECB EURINR 94.10 → base ₹4,563. Band ₹4,563–₹4,837. HDFC posts ₹4,751 on 14 Jun → inside band at +4.1%, which is exactly the expected markup. Amount points awarded, and ₹4,751 — the amount actually charged — is what goes to accounts. The euro figure is shown alongside as evidence only.

Amount loses weight because it is now an estimate; merchant name gains it, because a Lisbon restaurant descriptor on the HDFC alert is highly distinctive. Auto-match threshold is raised to 80 for foreign receipts.

Settlement lag is real. Foreign charges post 1–3 business days after the swipe, and a Saturday dinner in Prague can post the following Tuesday. Hence the asymmetric −1…+5 day window rather than ±7.

Everything else that differs outside India

Decimal formats
1.234,56 (DE/IT/ES/PT/NL) vs 1,234.56 (UK/IE) vs 1 234,56 (FR/CZ). Single biggest silent-error source. Explicit prompt rule + a deterministic reparse keyed to the detected country.
Date order
DD/MM across the EU, MM/DD if a US vendor sneaks in. Unambiguous when day > 12; otherwise resolved by country, and flagged for confirmation when it still can't be.
Tax identity
gstin generalises to tax_id + tax_id_type (GSTIN · EU_VAT · UK_VAT · other), with per-country VAT-number format validation.
Tax labels & languages
IVA · TVA · MwSt · BTW · MOMS · DPH · VAT. Receipts in PT/ES/FR/DE/IT/NL/CS. merchant_raw is kept verbatim; merchant is the normalised English form.

DCC — the easy case

If the terminal offered "pay in INR?" and you accepted, the slip prints both currencies. The model is told to capture the second amount as dcc_amount_inr — and then an exact match is available again, no FX modelling needed. (It also means you paid a worse rate; the bot quietly notes it.)

Markup & GST fee rows

HDFC often posts the markup fee and its GST as separate line items. These are auto-classified as fee, linked to the parent charge, and shown indented under it in the bundle — so accounts sees ₹4,751 + ₹166 markup + ₹30 GST as one story, not three orphan rows.

Reclaimable EU VAT

A Europe trip at 19–23% VAT on ~20 bills is real money. Once tax_breakdown is extracted, the bundle can carry a reclaimable VAT summary by country — the input your finance team needs for a 13th-Directive refund claim. Nearly free to generate; potentially the highest-value page in the document.

09 · CoreThe matching engine

Binding a receipt to the right charge

This is the part that makes or breaks the product. A wrong auto-match is worse than no match, so the engine is deliberately conservative: it only decides alone when the evidence is overwhelming, and it always leaves an undo.

Candidate window

Pull transactions for this user_id where is_refund = false, date within ±7 days of the receipt date, and amount within ±5% or ±₹20 of the receipt total. Typically 0–4 rows.

Weighted score (max 100)

SignalPtsRule
Amount40exact 40 · ±0.5% 32 · ±2% or ±₹5 22 · else 0
Date25same day 25 · ±1d 19 · ±2–3d 11 · ±4–7d 4

Amount is dominant on purpose. In practice the bank alert amount is authoritative and identical to the bill total; date and merchant exist to break ties, not to carry the decision.

Decision tree

AUTO
top ≥ 75 and (top − second) ≥ 15
Link it. Post the result card with an Wrong match undo button. matched_by='auto'.
▼
ASK
top ≥ 45, or a close runner-up
Show the top 3 as inline buttons with their scores plus "None of these". Your tap writes matched_by='user' — and is logged as training signal.
▼
DEFER
no candidate ≥ 45
Park as unmatched. Re-run automatically after every Gmail sync for 30 days — this is the common case when you photograph the bill before HDFC sends the alert.
▼
DUPLICATE
sha256 already seen
Reply with the existing match instead of creating a second row. Zero cost, no vision call.

The four hard cases, and how each is handled

Receipt before the alertDeferred queue + post-sync rematch hook in lib/sync.js. Solved without you doing anything.
Tip added after the slipBill ₹2,000, card charged ₹2,200. Amount band ±5% covers most tips; beyond that the bot asks and remembers the merchant as a "tip-prone" one.
One bill, split chargesTwo cards / part-cash. Bot offers Link to multiple — receipt_transactions is a join table, not a foreign key, precisely for this.
Two identical amounts, same dayNever auto-matched (gap rule blocks it). You pick from buttons; the merchant name on the slip usually makes it obvious.
Every decision is auditable. receipts.match_score, matched_by (auto|user|admin|rematch) and an app_logs entry are written for every link and every undo. If accounts ever queries a bundle, you can show exactly why receipt R-014 sits against charge #2841.

10 · BackfillTrips and the shoebox of old bills

The 20 European bills already sitting on your desk

Steady-state capture is one photo at a time. A past trip is the opposite problem: a stack of bills, all at once, against transactions that landed in the database weeks ago. Feeding those through the chat flow one by one would take an hour and defeat the point.

Bulk import path

1
Declare the trip. /trip Europe · 8–19 Jun 2026 in chat, or create it in the dashboard. Sets the date window and marks the receipts as travel.
2
Dump everything. Telegram media groups take 10 images per send; the dashboard takes an unlimited multi-file drop. Both land in the same queue.
3
Batch extraction. None of this is latency-sensitive, so it goes through the Batch API at 50% off — 20 receipts for about $0.04 — instead of 20 live calls.
4
Match against the trip window, with the foreign-currency scoring from page 08 and the trip's own transactions as the candidate pool. Far fewer false candidates than a blind ±7-day search.
5
One review screen, not 20 chats. A single table: proposed pairing, score, confidence, and every ambiguous one flagged. Confirm the lot in one pass, fix the two that are wrong.
Why a review screen and not chat here. Chat is right when the decision is one tap in the moment. Twenty simultaneous decisions want a table you can scan — this is precisely where the dashboard earns its place alongside the bot.

The card statement lands on the 18th. That is the anchor for everything: the cycle closes, the statement is reconciled against what the app collected, discrepancies get five days to resolve, and the verified bundle goes to accounts on the 23rd–24th.

12 · VerifyStatement ingestion & three-way reconciliation

The statement is the truth — everything else is a claim

Until now the app's picture of a month came only from email alerts, which is a lossy source: alerts get missed, sync gaps happen, refunds arrive quietly. The statement is the bank's own authoritative ledger. Reconciling against it turns the submission from "what we think you spent" into something that provably ties out.

18th · 01
Ingest
Gmail sync already matches hdfcbank senders, including emailstatements.cards@. The statement PDF is picked up automatically — no forwarding needed.
02
Decrypt
HDFC e-statements are password-protected. Opened with the stored password via a WASM qpdf build (works on serverless), then discarded from memory.
03
Parse
The decrypted PDF goes to Claude as a document, returning every line: date, description, amount, debit/credit, plus opening and closing balance.
04
Reconcile
Statement lines ↔ app transactions ↔ receipts. Every line lands in exactly one bucket below. Nothing is allowed to be unclassified.

The seven outcomes, and what each triggers

BucketExpectedAction
Tied out~85%Statement line matches a transaction and carries a receipt. Nothing to do.
On statement, missing from appa fewThe gap you can't currently see. The alert email never arrived or a sync window was missed. The transaction is created from the statement line, then chased for a receipt. This alone justifies the feature.
In app, not on statementoccasionalUsually a pre-auth that never settled, or a charge that will land in the next cycle. Rolled forward rather than deleted, with a note.
Refund / credit landedvariesMatched against the original charge and the expected-refund ledger. Marks that refund confirmed received.
Refund expected, not receivedthe pointAn Amazon return or a disputed charge where the credit has not appeared. Escalated in chat by name and age — "₹4,199 Croma return, 41 days, still not credited". These are the ones that quietly get lost.
Chargeback / disputerareTracked as its own state through raised → provisional credit → resolved, so a provisional credit is never mistaken for a settled one.
Feesevery cycleAnnual fee, forex markup, GST on markup, late fee. Auto-categorised as fee, linked to the parent foreign charge where applicable, and never chased for a receipt.

The control total — the thing that makes this auditable

opening balance + debits − credits − payments = closing balance. The reconciliation must reproduce the statement's own closing figure exactly. If it doesn't, the difference is shown to the rupee and the cycle is blocked from submission until it's explained.

This is the single strongest claim the bundle can make to accounts: not "here are some receipts", but "this reconciles to the bank's statement, and here is the proof line by line." That is the difference between a submission that gets processed and one that generates a thread of questions.

  user_id       text not null,

  tg_chat_id    bigint, tg_message_id bigint, tg_file_id text,
  storage_path  text not null,

  doc_type      text,
  merchant      text, merchant_raw text,
  amount        numeric, tax_total numeric,
  receipt_date  date, receipt_time text,
  -- international
  currency      text not null default 'INR',
  amount_inr    numeric, dcc_amount_inr numeric,
  fx_rate       numeric, fx_date date,
  country       text, city text, language text,
  tax_id        text, tax_id_type text, tax_breakdown jsonb,
  trip_id       uuid references trips(id),
  invoice_no    text, card_last4 text,
  extracted     jsonb, confidence numeric,
  status        text not null default 'pending'
                check (status in ('pending','matched',
                'unmatched','duplicate','rejected')),
  user_note     text,
  created_at    timestamptz default now(),
  unique (user_id, sha256)
);

-- the bank's own ledger; the spine of every submission
create table statement_lines (
  id             uuid primary key default gen_random_uuid(),
  statement_id   uuid not null references statements(id),
  line_no        int, txn_date date, post_date date,
  descriptor     text, amount numeric not null,
  type           text check (type in ('purchase','refund',
                 'fee','payment','chargeback')),
  transaction_id bigint references transactions(id),
  recon_status   text -- tied|created|orphan|unexplained
);
create index on receipts (user_id, status, receipt_date desc);

alter table transactions
  add column receipt_status text default 'missing',
  add column declared_reason text;

Design choices worth defending

Join table, not a foreign key. receipt_transactions allows one bill split across two charges and one charge covered by two documents (invoice + payment slip). Both happen.
Raw extraction is kept. extracted jsonb stores the model's full answer. When you improve the prompt in month three, you can re-derive fields from stored JSON without re-billing vision calls.
sha256 unique per user. The single cheapest defence against duplicate submissions — and it makes the bot's "you already sent me this" reply instant and free.
Materialised cycles. Submitted cycles freeze. Changing your statement day next year must not silently rewrite what accounts already received.
The statement outranks the app. statement_lines is the spine of a submission — transactions becomes the enrichment layer hanging off it, not the source of truth. A line with no matching transaction creates one; a transaction with no line is rolled forward. statements.tie_out_diff must read zero before anything can be sent.
Currency is first-class, not an afterthought. amount is always in the receipt's own currency; amount_inr is only an estimate — the authoritative rupee figure always lives on the transaction row. fx_rates is cached per currency-day so a lookup is never paid for twice.

Legacy has_receipt becomes a read-only view over receipt_status.

B · Supabase — the auth-issuer problem behind the anon key

Why RLS is effectively off today. The app authenticates with NextAuth + Google, not Supabase Auth. Supabase's auth.uid() reads a JWT signed by Supabase's own issuer — a NextAuth session carries no such token, so any policy written against it denies everything. The config that worked became "anon key + permissive policies", which is exactly why the daily cron can read every user's rows. Not a bug anyone introduced — the default outcome of mismatched issuers.
Fix: service-role + app-layer scoping. DO THIS
All DB access stays server-side on the service-role key; every query keeps its .eq('user_id', …) filter exactly as today; RLS is set to deny-all for anon as defence in depth. About an hour of work, zero behaviour change, and the cron's cross-user read becomes explicit and intentional rather than accidental.
If you want real RLS later. Sign a Supabase-compatible JWT in the NextAuth callback carrying sub = user_id, using the Supabase JWT secret — then auth.uid() works. ~30 lines, only needed if the browser should query Supabase directly.
Leaving Supabase entirely? Neon (Postgres, generous free tier, on the Vercel Marketplace) is the closest swap — but it migrates a working database for no functional gain. Fix the key model instead.

18 · TrustSecurity, privacy, auditability

This layer handles company financial records

Receipts carry GSTINs, card tails, vendor details and, for travel, personal itineraries. The bar is higher than for the dashboard, because this data leaves the system and lands in an accounts inbox.

Controls

Webhook authTelegram secret_token header compared in constant time; unknown chat_id silently dropped and logged. No open POST endpoint.
Account bindingOne-time codes from Settings, single-use, 15-minute expiry, one chat per user. Unlink button revokes instantly.
Object storagePrivate bucket. No public URLs anywhere. Access exclusively via signed URLs with a 15-minute TTL, issued only to the owning session.
Key hygieneService-role key is server-only and never reaches the browser bundle. Bot token never logged, never echoed in errors.
RLSRow-level security on every new table keyed to user_id; cron paths use the admin client explicitly and are the only cross-user readers.
Send gateOutbound email requires an explicit approval action tied to a submission id. No code path emails accounts automatically.
Audit trailapp_logs records every extraction, match, unmatch, declaration, bundle build and send — who, when, what score.
RetentionConfigurable. Default: originals kept 7 years (statutory GST record retention in India is 6 years from filing), extraction JSON kept indefinitely, Telegram file ids purged after binding.

Threats considered

Someone finds the bot. They can message it, but with no valid link code and an unknown chat id, every update is dropped before any work is done. No storage write, no vision call, no cost.
Webhook replay. update_id dedupe makes handling idempotent; a replayed photo resolves to the same sha256 and returns the existing match.
Wrong bundle to the wrong recipient. Recipients come from card_accounts, are shown in the approval card before sending, and changing them is a separate deliberate action.
Personal receipt sent by mistake. The bot flags any receipt whose extracted card_last4 disagrees with the configured company card and asks before filing it. Mixed personal spend on a company submission is a real audit problem.
Vision provider exposure. Receipt images are sent to Anthropic for extraction — the same trust boundary your bank alert emails already cross today in lib/sync.js. Worth stating explicitly to whoever signs off internally.
Pre-existing issue this surfaces. The current app authenticates Supabase with the anon key and the daily cron reads every user's rows — which only works because RLS isn't enforcing isolation. Phase 0 fixes that properly rather than building more on top of it.
